01 · Overview

ris3 is a fixed-supply ERC20 token on Ethereum mainnet paired with a Uniswap V4 pool and a separate leverage vault. Three contracts. No admin keys. No upgradability. No transfer hooks on the token.

The system is intentionally simple. A regular user buys or sells ris3 like any other ERC20 — through a Uniswap V4 pool with real concentrated liquidity. Pool indexers (DEXScreener, Sigma, Banana, etc.) see the pair as a standard V4 token and route swaps normally.

A separate vault contract holds an ETH treasury and offers 2× or 3× spot leverage to anyone who calls open(). The vault borrows from its own treasury to fund the leveraged buy, locks the resulting tokens as collateral, and tracks the debt. There is no external lending market, no interest model, no funding rate. Just an open fee on entry and the standard pool fees on the swaps themselves.

02 · Token

Ris3Token is a vanilla OpenZeppelin ERC20. The constructor mints the entire fixed supply (1,000,000 RIS3, 18 decimals) to the deployer in one call. No transfer hooks. No max-wallet. No admin role. No mint function. The contract is immutable post-deploy.

  • Symbol: RIS3
  • Decimals: 18
  • Total supply: 1,000,000 RIS3 (immutable)
  • Distribution at deploy: 100% to deployer wallet
  • Distribution after launch: deployer adds the entire 1M supply as single-sided V4 LP. No dev reserve.

03 · Pool & Hook

The ris3/ETH pair lives in a Uniswap V4 pool with the following PoolKey:

currency00x0 (native ETH)
currency1RIS3 token CA
fee10000 (1% LP fee tier)
tickSpacing60
hooksRis3EthFeeHook

The pool uses standard concentrated liquidity. Liquidity is provisioned by the deployer as a single-sided ris3 position spanning from tick -887220 (near-zero price) to a launch tick just below the initial spot price. As buyers move price up into the LP range, ETH accumulates and ris3 drains — standard Uniswap V4 mechanics.

The hook (Ris3EthFeeHook)

A V4 hook with the following permissions: BEFORE_SWAP, BEFORE_SWAP_RETURNS_DELTA, AFTER_SWAP, AFTER_SWAP_RETURNS_DELTA. It runs on every swap and skims 1% of the ETH side, routing the fee directly to the vault as native ETH:

  • Buy (ETH→RIS3): hook takes 1% of input ETH in beforeSwap, swap proceeds with 99% of input.
  • Sell (RIS3→ETH): hook takes 1% of output ETH in afterSwap, user receives 99% of output.

The hook does not override the swap math. Standard V4 LP curves resolve every swap. The hook only diverts a slice of the ETH leg. This is why indexers (DEXScreener et al.) see the pool as a normal token pair with light extra fees, similar to the LOTO and sato architectures.

04 · Leverage Vault

Ris3LeverageVaultV2 is the leverage layer. It holds an ETH treasury (deposited by its owner) and lends ETH to leverage positions. The vault has these key state variables:

  • treasuryEth — owner deposits + accumulated fees, minus withdrawals and liquidation shortfalls.
  • totalDebt — ETH actively lent across all open positions.
  • positions[id] — per-position record: owner, openBlock, collateral, debt.
  • availableTreasury() — withdrawable amount: max(treasuryEth − totalDebt, 0).

The owner can depositTreasury() and withdrawTreasury(amount) at any time, bounded by availableTreasury(). The vault has no other privileged functions. Anyone can call open(), close(id), liquidate(id), or sync().

05 · Position Mechanics

Opening (open)

Caller invokes vault.open(uint8 leverage, uint256 minCollateral) with msg.value as the margin. The vault:

margin = msg.value
debt = margin × (leverage − 1)
leveragedSize = margin × leverage
fee = leveragedSize × 0.5%
buyAmount = leveragedSize − fee

The vault checks availableTreasury() ≥ debt, then atomically:

  1. Credits fee to treasuryEth and debt to totalDebt.
  2. Calls Universal Router with value = buyAmount to execute a single ETH→RIS3 swap on the pool.
  3. Records the position: {owner, openBlock, collateral, debt}.

Because the vault funds the debt from its own ETH (rather than synthetic credit), every leverage open is a clean, single buy on the chart. There is no sell-then-buy round trip and no virtual reserve manipulation — the chart pumps by exactly the buy amount.

Closing (close)

The position owner calls vault.close(id, minProceeds). The vault:

  1. Requires block.number > position.openBlock (same-block-close shield).
  2. Sells the collateral via Universal Router. Measures proceeds as actual balance change.
  3. Reverts if proceeds < minProceeds.
  4. If proceeds ≥ debt: sends proceeds − debt to caller. The debt portion stays in the vault (returns to availableTreasury as totalDebt decreases).
  5. If proceeds < debt: caller gets nothing; treasuryEth absorbs the shortfall (debt − proceeds).
  6. Deletes the position.

Same-block close shield

A position cannot be closed in the same block it was opened. This blocks the classic open-pump-close sandwich pattern: open a leveraged position to push spot up, then close it immediately to capture the artificial pump on other trades in the same block. Reverts with SameBlockClose().

06 · Liquidation

Any address can call vault.liquidate(id, minProceeds) on any position. The vault sells the collateral and computes actual proceeds. Liquidation succeeds only if:

proceeds × 10000 < debt × 14000

That is, proceeds < 1.4 × debt. Otherwise liquidate reverts with PositionHealthy(). The 1.4× threshold leaves a 40% headroom over the debt — under normal market conditions, liquidators can act long before a position would actually leave the vault underwater. The minProceeds parameter is a sandwich-protection floor enforced in-block.

  • Bounty: min(proceeds × 1%, 0.01 Ξ) paid to the liquidator.
  • Remainder: goes to the vault treasury.
  • Shortfall (proceeds < debt + bounty): treasury absorbs the loss.

07 · Stop-Loss & Take-Profit

Every leveraged position carries two optional on-chain triggers, stored as uint160 sqrtPriceX96 thresholds against the live pool:

  • SL — fires when currentSqrtPriceX96 ≥ slSqrtPriceX96 (RIS3 cheaper than the floor).
  • TP — fires when currentSqrtPriceX96 ≤ tpSqrtPriceX96 (RIS3 above the target).

Set them at open via open(leverage, minCollateral, slSqrtPriceX96, tpSqrtPriceX96), or update later with setTriggers(id, sl, tp) — owner-only. A zero value disables that leg.

When a trigger condition holds, anyone can call closeOnTrigger(id). The vault reads slot0 from the V4 PoolManager, verifies the threshold, sells the collateral, repays debt, and pays the caller a 0.1% bounty (cap 0.001 Ξ) out of position proceeds. Remaining ETH goes to the position owner. Triggers cannot fire in the same block as open — same-block close shield applies to keep oracle-style flash manipulation off the table.
